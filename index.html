THE CLOSING OF THE FRONTIER

An Evidence-Based Investigation into the Disappearance of the American West

CONTEXT: For much of the 1800s, Americans used the word frontier to describe the shifting boundary between areas of established U.S. settlement and lands farther west that were less densely settled by non-Indigenous Americans. After the Civil War, railroads expanded rapidly across the continent. Settlers, businesses, farms, towns, and the U.S. government pushed farther west.

By 1890, the U.S. Census Bureau announced that there was no longer a clear frontier line. But what evidence shows that the frontier was actually disappearing?
